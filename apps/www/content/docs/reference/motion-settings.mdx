A theme sets how a product moves with six settings. Each is a CSS variable on `:root`. The defaults reproduce Ballmac UI exactly as it was, so adding the theme changes nothing until you change a setting. For what they do together, see [How motion works](/docs/concepts/motion).

## Settings [#settings]

| Variable | Range | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--bm-speed` | 0.5 to 2.5 | 1 | Playback speed. 1 is the default, 2 halves every duration, 0.5 doubles them. |
| `--bm-bounce` | 0 to 1 | 0.25 | Springiness, 0 (settles with no overshoot) to 1 (clearly bouncy). |
| `--bm-travel` | 0 to 2 | 1 | How far things travel when they enter or move, as a multiple of the default distance. |
| `--bm-hover` | 0 to 2 | 1 | How strongly controls answer a hover or press: lift, press depth. |
| `--bm-stagger` | 0 to 2 | 1 | The delay between items in a staggered list, as a multiple of the default step. |
| `--bm-ambient` | 1 or 0 | 1 | Decorative loops (shimmer, floating, pulsing). Off pauses them. |

Reduced motion has its own setting, `reduce`: "system" follows the visitor's setting. "always" ships the reduced values to everyone.

## Ready-made feels [#ready-made-feels]

Each is a registry theme that changes movement only and leaves your colors alone. Installing another replaces it.

```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @ballmac/motion-calm
```

### Native [#native]

The Ballmac default: short fades, a soft settle on spatial moves and a light lift on hover. Reads as the platform, not as an effect. Install: `@ballmac/motion-native`.

| Speed | Bounce | Travel | Hover | Stagger | Loops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.25 | 1 | 1 | 1 | On |

### Calm [#calm]

Longer, softer transitions with no bounce and short travel. Suits products people use for a long time, like editors, health and finance. Install: `@ballmac/motion-calm`.

| Speed | Bounce | Travel | Hover | Stagger | Loops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.75 | 0 | 0.7 | 0.5 | 1.4 | On |

### Snappy [#snappy]

Short durations, tight stagger and very little bounce. Suits dense tools and dashboards where speed is the feature. Install: `@ballmac/motion-snappy`.

| Speed | Bounce | Travel | Hover | Stagger | Loops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.6 | 0.05 | 0.7 | 0.8 | 0.5 | Off |

### Playful [#playful]

Clear overshoot, longer travel and a pronounced hover lift. Suits consumer apps, creative tools and marketing pages. Install: `@ballmac/motion-playful`.

| Speed | Bounce | Travel | Hover | Stagger | Loops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | 0.8 | 1.4 | 1.5 | 1.2 | On |

### Still [#still]

Ships the reduced-motion values to every visitor: no travel, no lift, no loops, no bounce. Opacity changes stay so state remains visible. Install: `@ballmac/motion-still`.

| Speed | Bounce | Travel | Hover | Stagger | Loops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.4 | Off |
