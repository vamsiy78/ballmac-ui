These items keep a fixed layout in right-to-left on purpose. For what mirrors and what doesn't, see [What mirrors in right-to-left](/docs/concepts/right-to-left).

- [Android Frame](/components/android-frame): Hardware buttons and bezels sit on fixed physical edges of the device, so the frame keeps its shape; the screen content you place inside follows the page direction.
- [Laptop Frame](/components/laptop-frame): Hardware buttons and bezels sit on fixed physical edges of the device, so the frame keeps its shape; the screen content you place inside follows the page direction.
- [Light Rays](/components/light-rays): Rays fan out from a point centred with left-1/2 and a negative margin; both are physical and symmetric, so the picture is the same in either direction.
- [Phone Frame](/components/phone-frame): Hardware buttons and bezels sit on fixed physical edges of the device, so the frame keeps its shape; the screen content you place inside follows the page direction.
- [Sparkles Text](/components/sparkles-text): Sparkles are placed by random percentages from the left edge and centred with a negative margin; decoration with no reading direction.
- [Tablet Frame](/components/tablet-frame): Hardware buttons and bezels sit on fixed physical edges of the device, so the frame keeps its shape; the screen content you place inside follows the page direction.
- [Watch Frame](/components/watch-frame): Hardware buttons and bezels sit on fixed physical edges of the device, so the frame keeps its shape; the screen content you place inside follows the page direction.
