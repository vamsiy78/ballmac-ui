### New

- [Status 1](/blocks/status-1) shows a banner worked out from the services, a 90-day history per service you scrub with a pointer or the arrow keys, uptime from the days, live incidents as a timeline and a subscribe box; you pass today yourself, the block never reads the clock.
- [Calculator 1](/blocks/calculator-1) estimates a price from sliders for seats, requests and storage, picks the cheapest plan that fits, itemizes the sum, and says "Talk to us" instead of inventing a price beyond your plans.
- [Survey 1](/blocks/survey-1) asks the 0 to 10 NPS question as big buttons (one tab stop, arrows choose), changes the follow-up with the answer, and keeps what was typed when a send fails.
- [Directory 1](/blocks/directory-1) searches people with highlighted matches, department chips with live counts and a location filter, and shows each person's availability in words and local time.
- [Waitlist 1](/blocks/waitlist-1) takes an email, shows the place in line, a share link with one-press copy, and a reward ladder that says in words which steps are unlocked.

### Improved

- The [MCP server](/docs/reference/mcp-tools) finds the new groups for uptime, incident, estimator, nps, feedback, people, employees, referral and launch.
- All five pass every scorecard check, including 360 px (checked to 320 px), reduced motion and right to left (the history bars in Status 1 stay left to right), and ship their tests.
