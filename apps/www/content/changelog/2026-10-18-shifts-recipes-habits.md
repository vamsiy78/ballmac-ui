### New

- [Roster 1](/blocks/roster-1) plans a week of shifts: set or change a shift in a dialog, see hours and overtime per person, a warning when rest between days is too short, and a coverage row ("3 of 4, short by 1").
- [Recipe 1](/blocks/recipe-1) scales every amount the way a cook reads it (½ and ¼ for spoons and cups), switches metric and US units, ticks off steps, and starts a timer that says "Time is up".
- [Habits 1](/blocks/habits-1) tracks streaks (kept alive until today is over), this week against a goal, the last seven days to tick and a twelve-week grid; a tick that fails to save goes back with the reason.
- [Inventory 1](/blocks/inventory-1) shows stock against reorder points, days left at today's pace and a status in words, with an order form that suggests how many to buy and says nothing was charged if it fails.
- [Trip 1](/blocks/trip-1) lays out a trip by day with copyable booking codes, anything unbooked marked in words, cost by type and per traveler, and a packing list with progress.

### Improved

- The [MCP server](/docs/reference/mcp-tools) finds the five new block groups for rota, shifts, ingredients, streak, warehouse, itinerary and vacation.
- All five pass every [scorecard check](/quality), including 360 px (checked to 320 px), reduced motion and right to left (the history grid in Habits 1 stays left to right), and ship their tests.
