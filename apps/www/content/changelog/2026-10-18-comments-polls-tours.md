### New

- [Comments 1](/blocks/comments-1) is a threaded discussion: type @ to mention people, reply, react with emoji, edit or delete your own comments, and resolve threads; a comment that cannot be saved stays with "Not posted" and Retry.
- [Poll 1](/blocks/poll-1) takes one choice or several up to a limit, counts your vote at once, marks the leader and your choice in words, and takes a failed vote back with the reason.
- [Tour 1](/blocks/tour-1) lifts one part of your interface out of a dimmed page while a card says what it is for. Point it at your own elements with `data-tour` attributes; focus is never taken on page load.
- [Portfolio 1](/blocks/portfolio-1) shows total value over a week, month or year on a line you scrub with the pointer or arrow keys, an allocation ring with a text equivalent and sortable holdings; gains and losses carry a sign and an arrow, not only color.
- [Seat map 1](/blocks/seatmap-1) picks seats on a hall with walkways and three price tiers, marks taken and wheelchair seats in words, finds neighbouring seats for a party, and keeps your other seats when one is taken.

### Improved

- The [MCP server](/docs/reference/mcp-tools) finds the new groups for discussion, vote, walkthrough, holdings, seats and cinema.
- All five pass every scorecard check, including 360 px (checked to 320 px), reduced motion and right to left (the floor plan in Seat map 1 stays left to right), and ship their tests.
