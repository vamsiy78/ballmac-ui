### New

- A Pro log in page at [/pro](/pro): the key stays in an encrypted cookie in your browser and is checked on every request, so a revoked key stops working within minutes. Buyers are logged in automatically when they return from checkout.
- Your Pro library has install commands with your key already filled in (hidden on screen, real on the clipboard) for the shadcn CLI, AI agents through MCP and the starter apps, plus browser downloads of Beacon SaaS, Quire and the Figma tokens. See [Set up Pro](/docs/guides/pro).
- Pro block pages show the real source with copy buttons once you are logged in, while the pages stay public and static for everyone else.
- A [support page](/support) for bugs, questions, licence and billing help, and suggestions; messages go to a private inbox and you get a confirmation by email.

### Improved

- A Log in and Get Pro button in the header becomes a Pro library link after you log in.

### Fixed

- Pricing copy corrected: Pro adds 150 blocks and two starter apps (it has no templates).
