# Templates batch C: content and personal

Reviewed 2026-10-01. Four content templates, each with its own art direction: Portfolio (a designer-developer's site), Studio (a loud agency site), Publication (an editorial magazine) and Podcast (a show with a transcript player). A new component, `audio-player`, was built for the podcast and ships on its own. All code is original Ballmac work assembled from components already in the registry; nothing was copied from Aceternity, Tailwind Plus (Spotlight, Pocket, Transmit), Magic UI Pro, shadcnblocks or any paid kit, so none carry a "Based on" header. They were compared with those kits, [shadcn blocks](https://ui.shadcn.com/blocks), [Magic UI](https://magicui.design) templates, [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/community/components), and the products themselves: Read.cv and Cosmos portfolios, Pentagram and Locomotive agency sites, The Atlantic, Aeon and Monocle for editorial layout, Apple Podcasts, Spotify and Transistor show pages.

What most template kits do: a portfolio that is a grid of identical cards, an agency page in generic gradient SaaS style, a blog with a sidebar, and a podcast page that is a list with a native audio tag. What these do instead:

| Template | Look | Signature interaction | Pages | What is better than the usual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portfolio | Bone paper, ink, one lime and a coral; Bricolage Grotesque with Geist Mono | A headline with inline colour pills, result cards that reveal on hover and keyboard focus, a skills marquee | Home, Work, Case study, Writing, Uses | Painted covers (no stock images), filter chips with a polite count, case study with a sticky fact sheet, table of contents and next project; search with an empty state; a footer with the email as a giant copy button |
| Studio | Stark white and black with orange, blue, pink and yellow blocks; Archivo on its width axis | A work reel whose poster follows the pointer and is also driven by keyboard focus | Home, Work, Project, Services, Contact | Hero type scales to the viewport without wrapping; grid or list view of work; services open as disclosures with price chips; a brief form that validates and focuses the first problem |
| Publication | Warm newsprint with oxblood; DM Serif Display, Lora, Public Sans | A masthead, reading progress, drop cap and margin notes that move under the text on narrow screens | Home, Article, Section, Author, Issues | Generated cover art in place of photos; section filter with sort and load more; footnote markers link to real notes; newsletter form with an error and a success state |
| Podcast | Candlelit cream and plum with amber; Young Serif, Nunito Sans | A sticky mini player that follows you between pages and an episode page whose transcript lines seek the audio | Home, Episodes, Episode, Hosts, Subscribe | Transcript search with highlights and follow-along; chapters on the scrubber; playback speed; support tiers as real radios; works with no audio file through a silent demo clock |

Shared decisions: each palette is a scoped block of standard Ballmac tokens in a `<style>` string for light and dark, also applied through `body:has(.template-theme)` so portaled UI inherits it. Text that sits on a bright accent uses a template variable (`--studio-on-accent`, `--publication-on-accent`, `--podcast-on-amber`) rather than `text-primary`, which flips in dark mode. Small coloured numbers and initials are set on filled chips so contrast is computed against a known background. Pages take an `hrefs` prop so previews navigate between sibling previews; dynamic routes (case study, essay, author) are hand-written. Data is fixed and deterministic.

audio-player: play and pause, back and forward skips, a Slider scrubber with chapter ticks, a speed cycle, a compact variant, controlled `time`/`onTimeChange`, and a silent clock when there is no `src` so previews and docs work offline. Compared with shadcn (no player), Magic UI and Vercel's Audio blocks (visual only), react-h5-audio-player (MIT, not accessible by default), and Wavesurfer (waveform, heavy): this one is keyboard operable, labels every control and respects reduced motion.

Accessibility notes: axe (serious and critical) is clean on every page in light and dark, and `scripts/a11y-open-states.ts` scans interacted states (hovered poster, list view, expanded service, form errors, newsletter error, load more, playing mini player, transcript search, selected support tier, audio-player playing and speed changed). Filters are `aria-pressed` buttons; scrollable regions are focusable and labelled; animation is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
